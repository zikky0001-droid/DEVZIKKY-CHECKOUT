DEVZIKKY PAY / SUPPORT

Help useful ideas keep moving.

Building a tool is only the beginning. Keeping it available, improving it, fixing bugs and testing new ideas all take time and resources. This page explains what support means for the DEVZIKKY TECH ecosystem—and what visitors can do today.

“A useful project deserves more than a launch day. It deserves the time and care needed to keep improving.”

WHY SUPPORT MATTERS

WHAT SUPPORT CAN SUSTAIN

Behind every public tool is ongoing infrastructure.

The DEVZIKKY portfolio's support notes identify hosting, serverless function usage, database usage, domain renewals, development infrastructure and future maintenance as areas where support may help.

☁

Hosting & availability

Hosting platforms and compute resources help keep project interfaces and backend services reachable.

▦

Serverless usage

APIs and backend functions can use platform resources as people interact with tools and features.

◫

Database services

Some applications depend on persistent storage, records, configuration and other database operations.

◎

Domains & renewals

Domains give projects a recognisable address and need to be renewed to remain under control.

⌘

Development resources

Testing, debugging, integration work and tooling take time and sometimes require paid infrastructure.

↻

Maintenance & improvement

Bug fixes, compatibility work, security updates and thoughtful improvements keep projects useful.

TRANSPARENCY FIRST

What a contribution would—and would not—mean.

01
It can help support project costs.

Examples include hosting, database use, domains, serverless resources and maintenance.

02
It does not purchase ownership.

A contribution does not transfer intellectual property, ownership or control of any project.

03
It is not a promise of a particular feature.

Support does not guarantee a release date, feature delivery, uptime level or priority access.

04
No unsupported allocation percentages.

This page does not claim a fixed percentage split or audited budget. Specific cost questions should be discussed before any future contribution is made.
