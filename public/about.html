DEVZIKKY PAY / ABOUT THE BUILDER

The person behind the projects.

DEVZIKKY is a student developer, technology enthusiast and builder from Nigeria. The work centres on turning curiosity into practical software: web applications, APIs, AI assistants, automation, bots, email tools and small utilities.

Student developerAndroid · TermuxWeb · APIs · AI
A HANDS-ON APPROACH

Start with an idea. Learn by making it real.

The portfolio describes an interest in taking an annoying or missing little thing and turning it into a working tool. That mindset leads naturally into experiments: a calculator, a quote generator, a mailbox, an AI assistant or a bot that automates a repetitive task.

Not every experiment needs to become a major product. The value is in learning what makes software useful: a clear problem, a thoughtful interface, predictable behaviour, careful handling of data and a willingness to keep fixing what breaks.

DEVZIKKY TECH brings these experiments together under one public identity so people can find the projects, inspect the work and follow future improvements.

“Notice something. Understand the problem. Build a solution. Test it. Improve it. Share it.”

THE DEVZIKKY DEVELOPMENT CYCLE

THE JOURNEY

From small experiments to a wider ecosystem.

The story in the portfolio is organised around learning through increasingly complex projects.

01

Start small — learn the foundations

Early experiments included calculators and simple web tools. Building small things made the basics of HTML, CSS and JavaScript more tangible.

02

Explore the backend

JavaScript, Node.js, Express, Python, APIs, databases and deployment became part of the learning process. The focus expanded from what a page looks like to how a service behaves.

03

Experiment with AI and bots

AI assistants, multiple model providers, text-to-speech, WhatsApp and Telegram automation opened up new ways to connect services and workflows.

04

Build complete systems

Email infrastructure, public tools, APIs, dashboards, authentication, rate handling and multi-project workflows now form part of the broader DEVZIKKY TECH collection.
